🔬 Cognitive Foundation: The VARK Model

Developed by Neil Fleming, the VARK inventory categorizes sensory modalities utilized for information processing:

Visual (V): Prefers information displayed as charts, diagrams, maps, and flowcharts.
Auditory (A): Learns best from verbal instruction, discussions, debates, and listening.
Read/Write (R): Maximizes retention via textual input, bulleted notes, essay writing, and bibliographies.
Kinesthetic (K): Retains information through tactile hands-on experience, lab experiments, and trial-and-error.
